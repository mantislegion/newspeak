Word-Formation Rules

Grammar Notes

- Newspeak strongly favors short, simple words.
- A root can often function as noun, verb, adjective, or adverb.
- Irregular verbs are regularized: thought → thinked, gave → gived, took → taked.
- Comparatives are regular: better → gooder, best → goodest.
- "whom" is replaced by "who". "shall" / "should" become "will" / "would".

Notice

This is a practical reference for translating ordinary English into Newspeak and back. Newspeak is not a one-to-one substitution cipher. It deliberately reduces vocabulary and nuance, so many sentences must be paraphrased rather than translated word for word.

Context matters more than a rigid dictionary. Where a concept has been removed from the language, such as privacy, the terminal will say so.
